Institutional Architecture

Built for Capital Protection & Asymmetric Upside

Operating under a dual-class equity structure (20% Class A Founder / 80% Class B Limited Partners), Emverse aligns management incentives directly with investor returns. Every deployment is governed by Constant Proportion Portfolio Insurance (CPPI) risk guardrails.

  • ✓ CPPI Principal Floor Protection: Hard capital floor of ₹2.488 Cr safeguarding debt service and operational continuity.
  • ✓ Tax-Efficient Structuring: Section 80M inter-corporate dividend pass-through and Section 80LA GIFT City FPI/NRI feeder readiness.
  • ✓ 20-Module Real-Time ERP: Live NAV accounting, automated waterfall distributions, and quarterly Capital Account Statements.

Distribution Waterfall Structure

Tier 1: Return of Capital 100% to LPs

All realized cash flows return 100% of contributed capital to Class B Limited Partners first.

Tier 2: Preferred Hurdle Rate 8.0% IRR

100% of profits distributed to LPs until an 8% compounded annual preferred return is achieved.

Tier 3: Carried Interest Split 80% LP / 20% GP

Residual profits above the hurdle are shared 80% to LPs and 20% to the General Partner.
