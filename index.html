About Emverse

Emverse Private Limited is a closely held asset holding entity incorporated under the Indian Companies Act, 2013. We pool capital to systematically invest across high-growth public & private equities, commercial real estate, clean energy infrastructure, and high-yield alternative assets in India.

Our objective is to provide a structured, institutional-grade corporate mechanism for long-term multi-asset wealth preservation and growth.

Multi-Asset Diversification

Strategic distribution of capital across public equities, real estate, sustainable energy, and agri-business models to balance capital growth with income generation.

Statutory & Governance Rigor

Operating strictly under the Indian Companies Act, 2013 with full corporate governance, statutory audits, and adherence to private placement limits.

NAV-Based Transparency

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Emverse Pvt Ltd a Mutual Fund or SEBI-registered AIF?

No. Emverse Private Limited is a closely held Private Limited Company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013. It is not a public investment fund, Collective Investment Scheme (CIS), or SEBI-registered Alternative Investment Fund (AIF). It operates purely as a private corporate entity investing its balance sheet capital.

How are fresh shares allotted to members?

Share allotments and debenture issuances are executed strictly on a private placement basis (under Section 42 of the Companies Act, 2013). New shares or Compulsorily Convertible Debentures (CCDs) are priced using independent Net Asset Value (NAV) valuations by a Registered Valuer.

How is liquidity or exit managed?

Shares carry a mandatory lock-in period as per our internal Shareholders' Agreement (SHA). Exits are facilitated through Right of First Refusal (ROFR) provisions where existing shareholders or the company buy back shares based on prevailing NAV.
