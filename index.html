Investor Governance & FAQ

Is Emverse Investments Private Limited a Mutual Fund or SEBI AIF?

No. Emverse Investments Private Limited is a closely held Private Limited Company under the Companies Act, 2013. It is not a public investment fund, CIS, or SEBI-registered AIF. It invests its own corporate balance sheet capital.

How are new shares allotted to incoming partners?

Allotments are made strictly via private placement (Section 42, Companies Act, 2013) based on Net Asset Value (NAV) valuation reports issued by Registered Valuers.

Investment Scope & Mechanism

At Emverse Investments Private Limited, our capital market vertical targets compounding long-term growth across public and private markets in India:

  • Listed Indian Equities: Direct equity positions in high-ROCE, fundamentally strong Indian businesses across banking, technology, manufacturing, and consumer sectors.
  • Unlisted & Pre-IPO Shares: Strategic acquisitions of pre-IPO shares in tech and consumer unicorns through corporate transfer forms (SH-4) prior to public listing.
  • Mutual Fund Core: Core positions in flexi-cap and index mutual funds to provide a resilient, liquid foundation.
