Investor Governance & FAQ

Is Emverse Investments Private Limited a Mutual Fund or SEBI AIF?

No. Emverse Investments Private Limited is a closely held Private Limited Company under the Companies Act, 2013. It is not a public investment fund, CIS, or SEBI-registered AIF. It invests its own corporate balance sheet capital.
